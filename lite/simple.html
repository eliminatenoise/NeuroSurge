NeuroSurge Lite · Simple guide

Play NeuroSurge Lite

You play the same game in Lite and Full. The same keys and mouse actions move the body, turn the view and select parts of the brain. The player guide lists those actions. The body and its brain activity are part of a computer simulation.

Download the same shapes in less space

The body and brain surfaces are made of small triangles. A mesh is the list of those triangles and their corner positions. Lite downloads the meshes in smaller files. Think of the smaller files as a compact way to write the same list. When the browser opens them, every corner and triangle is in the same place as in Full. The 611 mesh records contain 1,048,620 triangles. The same body rig supplies the joint positions and directions, and the same material settings give the surfaces their appearance.

The five packs, their contents list and the body rig take 5,995,969 bytes in Full and 4,999,707 bytes in Lite. A byte measures the size of the files. The format that makes these files smaller is called Brotli. If your browser cannot unpack that format itself, Lite loads a small program to do it on your computer. That program adds 142,385 bytes. The packs, list, rig and unpacking program together use 5,142,092 bytes in that case.

Play a copy on your computer

  1. Download NeuroSurge Lite if you have not already. Extract the ZIP and keep all its files and folders together.
  2. Install Node.js 18 or newer if you need it. In the extracted lite folder, open Play NeuroSurge Lite on Windows.cmd on Windows or Play NeuroSurge Lite on macOS.command on a Mac. On Linux, open a terminal in that folder and enter sh "Play NeuroSurge Lite on Linux.sh".
  3. To start from the terminal, open it in the main folder you extracted and run node lite/launch.mjs. The launcher serves the game on your own computer. If the browser stays closed, copy the address the launcher prints and paste it into a browser that supports WebGL, the browser feature that draws the 3D bodies. Choose Play Lite.
  4. Keep the terminal open while playing. When you finish, press Ctrl+C there to stop the server.
