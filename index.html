First moves
  1. Move the mouse to look around when the game has captured the pointer. With a free pointer, drag on empty space. Aim at a coloured area of the brain to move the named body part. Click to repeat the movement.
  2. Press 1, 2, 3 or 4 to select a limb. Use W A S D to move it. In the eye view, a small look up or down raises or lowers it at the shoulder or hip.
  3. Press E to change view, M to see the electrical map and Esc to pause or open the tutorial.
