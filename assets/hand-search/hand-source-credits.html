Hand replay sources and reuse

Xin Wang, Taein Kwon, Mahdi Rad, Bowen Pan, Ishani Chakraborty, Sean Andrist, Dan Bohus, Ashley Feniello, Bugra Tekin, Felipe Vieira Frujeri, Neel Joshi and Marc Pollefeys released the HoloAssist dataset with their ICCV 2023 paper. Dataset and paper.

The joint positions and action sentences in this replay derive from R031-12July-SmallPrinter, a training recording. The source stores a 4 by 4 pose matrix per joint. We use the three translation entries from the final column. The file supplies an active hand flag and two flags per joint for validity and tracking. A position enters the replay only when all three flags admit it and its coordinates are finite. The 26 names follow the original joint order. The guide lines are drawn between pairs of available joints. HoloAssist projection code · Joint definitions.

The export keeps the coordinate axes of the Psi recording system. Its sensor components use a world anchor. The HoloAssist projection example applies inverse camera pose to place these positions in the camera frame. The replay uses the exported positions directly. It shows no room or independently located headset. Numeric coordinates are rounded to the nearest millionth of a metre for storage. The headset’s physical tracking error was not measured here. The publisher synchronises the hand file to RGB video. The native hand sensor ran at 43.2 Hz on average across the study, and the released right-hand file for this recording has 4,159 RGB-aligned rows from 0 to 138.6 seconds. Playback uses the archived relative time of each row. Each sample also carries a clock reading in units of 100 nanoseconds relative to the original file’s first sensor timestamp. Some neighbouring rows share this reading. The event interval includes its start and excludes its end. Acquisition and annotation methods.
