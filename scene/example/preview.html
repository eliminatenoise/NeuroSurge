A right wrist points to a target

The participant saw a target on a screen. Its visual go marker sets time zero. Our description is “bend the right wrist toward the palm.” The encoder moved from 2.6° of extension at the cue to 49.3° of flexion 2.5 seconds later.

Scalp voltage at C3, a surface electrode pair over the right flexor carpi radialis, and two angles from the wrist encoder. Negative extension means flexion. The dotted line marks the go cue. EMG measures electrical activity; muscle force and sensory nerve firing were unmeasured.

The hold forecast repeats the last encoder reading. The fitted forecasts use cue time with wrist history, then with scalp EEG added. This single development trial illustrates their errors. It cannot establish accuracy on other people or tasks.

Where the recording comes from

WristMotion v3 · sub-41 · run 03 · first pointing trial. All four traces use the same stored 512 Hz clock. The encoder updates at 100 Hz; physical timing offsets between instruments remain unknown. The plotted interval runs from 0.50 s before the visual go marker through 2.50 s after it. These are offline readings of a human right wrist.
