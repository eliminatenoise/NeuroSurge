# Sources and rights

Jorge German Perez Blanco and Javier Mauricio Anteliz Ortiz deposited the **EEG-EMG-WristMotion Dataset, version 3**, under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. NeuroTranslation grouped earlier encoder angles and scalp voltages into short averages, derived four forearm amplitudes from paired electrode differences, and fitted and evaluated right-wrist forecasts. The results, training scales and fitted parameters carry this attribution and the source’s CC BY 4.0 terms wherever applicable. The source recordings remain with the authors’ deposit. [Version 3 dataset](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.29666735.v3) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) · [Study descriptor](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-026-07287-z)

The project dedicates its original code, documentation and invented input to CC0. The repository's shared learner and alignment checks are included as source. The numerical example output is the result of running the fitted models on the invented input. PyTorch and NumPy are separately installed dependencies under their respective terms.
